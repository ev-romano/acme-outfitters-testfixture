Started in a garage foundry

Acme Outfitters began when our founder, Dana Okafor, spent a decade buying cast-iron pans that warped, cracked, or came so poorly seasoned they rusted in a month. After the tenth bad pan, Dana started casting iron in a garage foundry with a single goal: make one skillet worth keeping for life.

Why only one pan

We could make a dozen sizes and a wall of accessories. We don't. A focused shop makes a better product than a broad one. Every hour we would spend on a second SKU goes into getting the one skillet right and into helping you keep the one you already own.

How we talk to you

Plainly, and without hype. We won't rush you with fake urgency or a countdown timer. We'd rather you buy one pan once than replace a cheap one every year. Warm, direct, a little bold, never chatty.
