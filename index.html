The last skillet you'll buy

We make a single 10-inch cast-iron skillet, pre-seasoned by hand in our foundry and guaranteed for life. We don't do collections, seasonal drops, or a catalog of pans in five sizes. We make one, we make it right, and we help you keep it for decades.

Our whole philosophy is repair before replace. If your skillet ever chips, warps, or loses its season, we'll restore it rather than sell you a new one.

What we believe

  • One product, done right. Focus beats a bloated catalog.
  • Repair before replace. The greenest pan is the one you already own.
  • Made to be handed down. Cast iron outlives its owner when it's cared for.

Who it's for

Home cooks who keep their tools for twenty years, and gift-buyers looking for something that will still be in the family long after the wrapping paper is gone.
