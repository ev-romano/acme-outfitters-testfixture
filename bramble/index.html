Nine seasons of trail-crew dinners

Rowan Pike spent nine seasons cooking for trail crews and river trips, feeding twelve people a night off a fire grate. The gear kept losing: pans warped, handles melted, kits rattled apart. In 2019 Rowan started sewing waxed-canvas utensil rolls on a secondhand machine in a converted hay barn outside Hood River. The skillet came next.
