Start with the honest answer

Carbon steel changes color and needs oil. Enamel can chip on rock. A kettle used on flame collects soot. Our Folding Fire Grate weighs 9.5 lb, which makes it steady at a drive-in camp and a poor companion on a long walk. We would rather tell you before you order than surprise you at the trailhead.

When a problem does turn up, start with a photograph and a few plain details. Manufacturing defects on metal goods are covered for life. Rust and uneven skillet seasoning are often repairable, and we will talk you through the work or reseason the pan free. You send it to us; we send it back. Canvas rolls can come home for free re-waxing for life, with return shipping on us.

Planning an order

Orders leave Hood River, Oregon, within 1–2 business days, Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays. US standard shipping is free at $75 or more and $7 below that mark. Canada shipping is $18. The Fire Grate cannot go to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses, though everything else can ship to APO/FPO.

Still unsure? Email hello@bramble-supply.example or call (541) 555-0142, Monday–Friday, 8am–4pm Pacific. We reply within one business day. Our small Hood River barn pickup counter is also open Saturdays 10–2. For the address and a message form, visit contact.
