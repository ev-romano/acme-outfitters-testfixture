Returns and exchanges

Take it outside. Cook a meal. If the gear is not right for you, new or lightly used items can come back within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

What lightly used means

We will take back a skillet you have cooked in once or twice. A little soot, a first layer of seasoning, or the marks from a careful trial do not disqualify honest gear. We cannot take back equipment that has been through a season of use, is missing parts, or has been altered or damaged through misuse.

Pack the item clean, dry, and complete. Include the canvas sleeve or tools that came with it. This protects the next person opening the box and helps us process the return without chasing loose pieces around the barn.

Start a return

  1. Email hello@bramble-supply.example with your order number, the item, and whether you want a refund or exchange.
  2. We reply within one business day with instructions. For US returns, we send a free prepaid label. Customers in Canada arrange and pay for return shipping.
  3. Pack the item securely and drop it with the named carrier. Keep the receipt until the return is complete.

Returns go to Bramble Supply, 1140 Orchard Road, Hood River, OR 97031. Please contact us before sending a parcel so we know why it is arriving.

Refunds and exchanges

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return reaching the barn. Your bank may need additional time to display the credit. For an exchange, return shipping is free and we ship the replacement as soon as the original return is scanned by the carrier. That gets the right gear moving before the wrong gear finishes its trip home.

Final sale

Gift cards and items marked “Barn Seconds” are final sale. Barn Seconds have cosmetic blemishes that do not prevent use; the specific blemish should be described before purchase. Final sale does not erase our responsibility for a covered manufacturing defect.

Returns are not the guarantee

The 60-day return window lets you decide whether gear suits you. The Built for the Long Haul guarantee covers manufacturing defects for the stated life of the product. Rust or patchy seasoning on carbon steel is usually a care issue, but we still help: we can walk you through a fix or reseason it free. You ship the skillet to us, and we ship it back.

Was the order damaged during shipping? That has its own fast path: email a photo within 14 days and we send a replacement. Full details are on the shipping page.
