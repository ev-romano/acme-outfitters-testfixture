Frequently asked questions

Short answers from the barn. Need another? Ask us.

Where is Bramble Supply gear made?

Skillets and fire grates come from a family-run Ohio metal shop. Ember tongs are forged in Oregon. Enamelware is made in Portugal. Canvas rolls are sewn in our Hood River barn. The Trail Kettle is made in Taiwan by our long-time partner factory.

Does food release easily from The Camp Skillet?

It can with sound seasoning, fat, and sensible heat, but food can still stick. The cooking surface improves through use and care.

Can the skillet go in a dishwasher?

No. Dishwasher detergent and a long wet cycle can strip seasoning and invite rust. Wash it promptly, dry it over low heat, and wipe on a very thin coat of oil.

Which heat sources work with the skillet?

Use it on a campfire grate, over coals, or on gas, electric, and induction ranges. It also goes in the oven. Its steel handle gets hot everywhere.

Why is my skillet changing color?

Carbon steel develops a dark, uneven patina. Blue, brown, and black are normal. Orange, powdery patches are rust; scrub and reseason them.

Can the enamel mugs sit over a flame?

Yes, when they are not empty. Liquid helps moderate heat. The mug and handle become hot. The enamel plates are not cookware and should stay off direct flame.

Will enamel chip?

It can. Enamel is glass fused over a steel core. A fall onto rock or a hard impact can chip it, which is wear rather than a manufacturing defect.

Does fire ruin the kettle’s finish?

Fire will darken the stainless base with soot. That change is cosmetic. Wash off loose carbon or leave the working patina alone; either choice boils the same water.

Is the Folding Fire Grate for backpacking?

Probably not. At 9.5 lb, it suits a drive-in kitchen, river camp, or base camp. It folds to 1.5 inches flat.

Can you ship the grate to my PO box or APO/FPO address?

No. A carrier restriction keeps the Fire Grate from PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses. Everything except the grate can ship to APO/FPO.

How quickly will my order leave?

Orders ship from Hood River within 1–2 business days, Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays. Expedited orders placed by 11am Pacific ship the same day.

When is US standard shipping free?

US standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. It is $7 flat below $75, with delivery estimated at 2–6 business days after shipping.

Do you ship outside the United States?

We ship to Canada for $18 flat, with delivery in 6–12 business days. Duties and taxes may be collected on delivery and are the buyer’s responsibility. We do not ship elsewhere yet.

Can I return a skillet after cooking in it?

Yes. New or lightly used gear may be returned within 60 days of delivery, and a skillet used once or twice qualifies. Gear used through a season does not.

Who pays return shipping?

US returns get a free prepaid label. Canadian customers pay return shipping. Exchanges are free; replacements ship when the return is scanned.

What is covered for life?

Manufacturing defects on metal goods: The Camp Skillet, Folding Fire Grate, Forged Ember Tongs, and The Trail Kettle. Normal wear, soot, seasoning changes, and misuse are not defects.

Can Bramble fix a rusty skillet?

Yes. We can talk you through rust rescue or reseason it free. You pay to ship the skillet to us, and we pay to ship it back.

Will you re-wax my utensil roll?

Yes. Re-waxing is free for life. Send the empty roll to the barn and we cover return shipping. Contact us before mailing it.

Can I pick up an order?

Our small barn pickup counter at 1140 Orchard Road in Hood River is open Saturdays 10–2. Bring your order number.

What happens if my order arrives damaged?

Email a photo within 14 days of delivery. Include the box and item if possible, and we will send a replacement.
