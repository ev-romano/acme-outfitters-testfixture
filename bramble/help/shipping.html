Shipping without the mystery

We pack every order in our Hood River barn and hand it to the carrier within 1–2 business days, Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays.

Expedited orders placed by 11am Pacific ship the same day. Orders placed after that cutoff leave the next business day. Weather, road closures, carrier interruptions, and remote destinations can still slow a parcel after it leaves us.

Where we ship

We currently ship only to the United States and Canada. Canadian duties and taxes may be collected on delivery and are the buyer’s responsibility. We do not hide those charges in the product price, and we cannot predict the amount charged by local authorities.

APO/FPO addresses are welcome for everything except the Folding Fire Grate. The Fire Grate also cannot ship to a PO box because of a carrier restriction. Give us a street address for that item. If a grate and smaller goods share an order with an ineligible address, contact us before ordering so we can help sort the shipment.

Tracking and delivery

We email tracking when the label prints. A label may need a little time to receive its first carrier scan. Check the delivery address and any apartment or unit number in the confirmation email. If it is wrong, contact us quickly; we can change it only before the parcel is handed off.

For holiday planning, order by December 15 for standard delivery by December 24 in the contiguous US. That date is our practical cutoff, not an invitation to make the carrier race a snowstorm.

If the box arrives damaged

Email hello@bramble-supply.example with a photo within 14 days of delivery. Include the order number, the shipping box, packing materials, and damaged item in the pictures if possible. We will send a replacement. Keep the packaging until we reply in case the carrier asks for another view.

For an ordinary return rather than transit damage, use our returns and exchanges guide. For anything unusual, contact the barn; we reply within one business day.
