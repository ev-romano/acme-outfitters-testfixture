The Camp Skillet · 3/5

“It cooked beautifully, but orange spots showed up after I left it drying overnight. I expected less fuss from a camp pan.”

— Devon B., Missoula, Montana

Rowan replied: Carbon steel needs to be dried fully and oiled lightly, especially after camp washing. The spots are fixable. We sent Devon our rust-rescue steps and offered free reseasoning if the pan made the trip back to the barn.

Speckle Enamel Mugs · 2/5

“Mine chipped at the base when it slipped onto a stone. It still works, but I thought camp enamel would shrug that off.”

— Amina G., Spokane, Washington

Rowan replied: We are sorry the first outing ended with a chip. Enamel is glass over steel and can chip on rock; impact damage is not a defect. We have made that limit clearer and offered Amina help checking that the exposed edge was smooth and safe to keep using.
