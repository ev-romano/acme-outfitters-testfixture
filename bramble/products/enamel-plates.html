What it is

Four proper dinner plates for picnic tables, tailgates, and fireside suppers. They are made by a third-generation enamel works in Portugal with a steel core, glass enamel, and a rolled stainless rim.

How to use it

The deep rim corrals beans, eggs, and food cut with a pocketknife. Stack them flat in the chuck box with a cloth between them on rough roads.

Care

Dishwasher safe. Hand washing with a soft sponge keeps the finish looking fresh. Soak stubborn camp food instead of reaching for steel wool.

Good to know

Enamel is glass fused to steel. It can chip when struck or dropped on rock. These are plates, not cookware; keep them off direct flame.

Guarantee

Enamelware is covered for 1 year against manufacturing defects; impact chips are wear. See returns and our guarantee.
