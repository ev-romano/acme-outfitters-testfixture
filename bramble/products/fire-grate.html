What it is

A broad, stable cooking deck formed and riveted by a family-run Ohio metal shop. Four legs fold out for cooking and tuck in for the drive home. A canvas sleeve contains the soot.

How to use it

Open every leg fully on mineral soil inside an established fire ring. Bank coals beneath one side to make hot and warm zones. Let the grate cool completely before folding it.

Care

Brush off food and ash, dry it, and wipe bare spots lightly with cooking oil. Store dry in the sleeve. Surface color will change with heat.

Good to know

At 9.5 lb, this is drive-in gear, not a backpacking grate. It cannot ship to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses. Obey local fire restrictions and burn bans.
