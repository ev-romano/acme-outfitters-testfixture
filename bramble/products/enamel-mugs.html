What it is

A set of two sturdy camp mugs from a third-generation enamel works in Portugal. A steel core, glass enamel, and rolled stainless rim make them cheerful without making them precious.

How to use it

Fill with coffee, cocoa, soup, or water before setting over a camp stove or fire. Keep the handle where you can reach it safely and lift with protection; the mug and handle get hot.

Care

Wash by hand or in the dishwasher. For soot, soak first and use a non-scratch pad. Do not scour with steel wool.

Good to know

Stovetop and fire safe only when not empty. Glass enamel can chip if dropped on rock. A chip from impact is wear, not a manufacturing defect.

Enamelware is covered for 1 year against manufacturing defects. See returns and our guarantee.
