Privacy policy

This fictional test storefront collects only the information needed to run the shop, answer questions, and improve the site. This policy explains the practical details.

Information we collect

When you place or discuss an order, we may receive your name, email, phone number, billing and shipping address, order contents, payment status, and messages. Payment card details are handled by the checkout payment provider and are not stored in full by Bramble Supply. If you use our contact form, we receive the fields you submit, including any order number and message.

Our site systems may also collect device and browser information, IP address, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location, and cookie or similar identifiers. These records help the storefront function, prevent fraud, understand broad traffic patterns, and diagnose errors.

How we use information

  • Process, ship, and support orders.
  • Send transactional messages such as confirmations, tracking, return instructions, and service notices.
  • Answer product, care, guarantee, and pickup questions.
  • Detect abuse, secure the storefront, and meet legal or accounting duties.
  • Send marketing email or SMS only when you have given the required consent.

Consent to marketing is optional and is not a condition of purchase. You can unsubscribe from email through the message link. You can opt out of SMS by replying STOP. Message and data rates may apply. We do not sell personal information for money.

When information is shared

We share the minimum needed with service providers that support payment processing, hosting, analytics, email, fraud prevention, shipping, and customer service. Carriers receive delivery details. Providers may use information only to perform their work for us, subject to their own legal obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

Retention and security

We retain order and tax records as needed for business and legal purposes. Support messages are kept while useful for resolving an issue and improving service. Marketing preferences remain until changed. No online system is perfectly secure, but we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and limit access to people who need it.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information, or to object to certain uses. Some records may be retained when law or a legitimate business need requires it. Browser settings can limit cookies, though essential storefront features may then work poorly.

Contact and updates

For a privacy request, email hello@bramble-supply.example or write Bramble Supply, 1140 Orchard Road, Hood River, OR 97031. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We may update this policy as the test storefront changes; the version posted here governs current use. Questions about ordinary orders belong on our contact page.
