Good food, sensible fire

Every fire lesson begins with the same check: are fires allowed here, today? Follow local restrictions, burn bans, campground rules, and Leave No Trace guidance. When flame is not permitted, use an allowed camp stove or change the menu. Breakfast will forgive you.
