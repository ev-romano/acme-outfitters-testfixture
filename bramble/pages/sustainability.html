Home / Materials and responsibility

Leave it better

A camp kitchen still uses steel, energy, freight, and packaging. We will not call that impact-free. We can make durable gear, name its origins, reduce waste, and put money back into the trails that carry us.

1% for trail crews

We give 1% of sales to trail-maintenance crews in the Pacific Northwest. That commitment is based on sales, not profit. Crews clear fallen trees, repair drainage, harden fragile tread, restore campsites, and do the slow work that keeps a route usable through wet winters and busy summers.

Money is only part of leaving it better. In camp, we follow Leave No Trace: use established sites, respect fire restrictions and burn bans, keep food and wash water managed, pack it in and pack it out, and leave cold ash and a clean ring according to local rules.

Made where the work fits

We choose makers for their experience, not for a tidy marketing sentence. The Camp Skillet and Folding Fire Grate are spun or formed and riveted at a family-run metal shop in Ohio. Forged Ember Tongs are hand-forged by a blacksmith in Oregon. Waxed Canvas Utensil Rolls are sewn by our team in the converted hay barn outside Hood River.

Our enamel mugs and plates are made in Portugal by a third-generation enamel works. They build a steel core, fire on glass enamel, and finish the edge with a rolled stainless rim. The Trail Kettle is made in Taiwan from 18/8 stainless steel by a long-time partner factory. Taiwan is not a footnote: it is where skilled people make this kettle, and we are honest about it.

Long use beats quick replacement

“Built for the long haul” means choosing materials that can work for years and helping owners maintain them. Carbon steel can be scrubbed and reseasoned. Bare steel can be dried and oiled. Canvas can be re-waxed. We re-wax our canvas rolls free for life, and we reseason a Camp Skillet free when you send it to us.

No material is magic. Enamel can chip. Steel can rust. Stainless gathers soot. Care, repair, and honest limits help keep small trouble from becoming discarded gear. Our guarantee covers manufacturing defects, while the care guide handles the everyday scars.

What arrives at your door

We pack orders in recycled cardboard with paper tape. We do not use plastic foam. Packaging still consumes material, so we size boxes closely and avoid decorative layers whose only job is to be thrown away. Reuse the box for storage or shipping, then recycle it according to local rules.

We are a team of 9, and there is more to improve. If you spot waste we can reasonably remove or have a question about a maker, write to the barn. Useful scrutiny is welcome.
