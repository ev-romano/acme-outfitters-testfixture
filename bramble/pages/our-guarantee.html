Built for the Long Haul

Buy once. Repair it. Pass it on. Our guarantee covers failures in materials or workmanship for the periods below, and our first instinct is to fix useful gear.

What is covered

Waxed Canvas Utensil RollStitching and hardware2 years

A manufacturing defect is a problem in the material or the way an item was made: a rivet that lets go in ordinary use, a seam that unravels without being cut, or enamel that arrives improperly fired. We may ask for the order number and clear photos so we can understand what failed.

What use looks like

Outdoor cooking leaves marks. Carbon steel darkens, develops uneven seasoning, and may rust if stored damp. Stainless steel collects soot. Matte steel changes color near heat. Waxed canvas creases and lightens at folds. Enamel may chip after a fall or impact. Those are wear or care issues, not manufacturing defects.

The guarantee does not cover loss, theft, misuse, neglect, modifications, damage from using gear contrary to its instructions, or ordinary wear. Enamel chips caused by drops or impact are specifically not defects. We will still offer practical advice when coverage does not apply.

Repair first

A rusty Camp Skillet or patchy season is usually recoverable. We will walk you through rust removal and seasoning, or reseason the skillet free. You pay to ship it to the barn; we ship it back. For the Waxed Canvas Utensil Roll, re-waxing is free for life. Send the empty roll in and we cover return shipping.

When a covered item fails, we decide with you whether repair or replacement makes the most sense. A replacement may show small differences as materials and production methods improve, but it will do the same job.

Make a claim

  1. Email hello@bramble-supply.example with your name, order number if available, product, and a clear description.
  2. Include photos of the whole item and the problem area.
  3. Wait for shipping instructions before mailing anything. We reply within one business day.

This guarantee is separate from our 60-day return policy, which covers new or lightly used gear that simply is not right for you. It also does not replace rights you may have under applicable consumer law.
