Frequently asked questions

Help center · Store home

Do you make other sizes?

No. We make one 10-inch skillet. It's the size that handles a weeknight dinner for two to four and still fits most ovens and grills.

Does it work on induction?

Yes. Cast iron is naturally magnetic, so the skillet works on induction, gas, electric, in the oven, and over a campfire.

Is it really pre-seasoned?

Yes, by hand with flaxseed oil. You can cook on it the day it arrives. The season deepens the more you use it.

What is the warranty?

A lifetime guarantee. If the skillet cracks or warps, or you can't restore the season, we repair or re-season it for free. See returns & the lifetime guarantee.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes, and most orders in the last quarter of the year are gifts. Add a note at checkout and we'll include a handwritten card and leave the price off the slip.

How do I keep it from rusting?

Dry it on the stove after washing and rub in a drop of oil. Full steps are in the cast-iron care guide.
