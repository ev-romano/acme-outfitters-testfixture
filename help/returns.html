90-day returns

If the skillet isn't for you, send it back within 90 days for a full refund. Used is fine, seasoned is fine. We re-season returned pans and offer them at a discount rather than throwing anything away.

The lifetime guarantee

Every skillet is guaranteed for as long as you own it. Cracks, warping, or a season you can't recover are all covered.

Repair before replace

This is the part we care about most. When something goes wrong we try to repair or re-season your pan before we ever send a new one. Most problems, including heavy rust, are fixable. Email us a photo and we'll send a prepaid label, restore the pan, and send it home, usually within two weeks.

How to start

Email hello@acme-outfitters.example with your order number and a photo. A real person replies, no return-merchandise robot.
