How long it takes

We ship within two business days of your order. Delivery inside the US then takes three to five business days. We'll email tracking as soon as it's on the way.

What it costs

Shipping is free everywhere in the US. International shipping is a flat rate of 20 dollars, and any import duties are the buyer's responsibility.

Where we ship

We ship to the US, Canada, the UK, and the EU. If your country isn't on that list, email us and we'll see what we can do.

The skillet is heavy

Cast iron is dense, so the box is heavier than it looks. It ships in a right-sized recycled carton with no plastic filler.
