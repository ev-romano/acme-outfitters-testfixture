Cast-iron care guide

Everyday washing

Rinse the skillet under warm water while it's still warm and wipe it with a brush or a non-scratch pad. A little soap is fine on a well-seasoned pan. Never leave it to soak.

Drying and oiling

Dry it right away, then set it on a low burner for a minute to drive off the last moisture. Rub in a single drop of neutral oil while it's warm and buff it until it looks dry, not greasy. This is what keeps rust away.

Re-seasoning

If food starts to stick or the surface looks dull, re-season it: coat it thinly with oil, wipe most of it back off, and bake it upside down at 450°F for an hour. Let it cool in the oven. Repeat for a deeper season.

Rescuing a rusty pan

Rust is not the end. Scrub the rust off with a stiff brush and a little coarse salt, wash, dry completely, and re-season as above. If it's badly rusted, send it to us under the lifetime guarantee and we'll restore it for free.

What to avoid

Skip the dishwasher, don't store it damp, and don't leave acidic food (tomato sauce, wine) sitting in it overnight. Everything else it can take.
