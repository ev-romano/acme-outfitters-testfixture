The Braiser — 3.5 qt, $150

Add to cart Free US shipping · Ships in 2 business days

What it is

A wide, shallow, 3.5-quart cast-iron pan with two loop handles and a heavy cast-iron lid. It sits between our skillets and the Dutch Oven: wide enough to sear six chicken thighs in one layer, deep enough to finish them in a simmering sauce, and low enough to go straight under the broiler to crisp the skin. Cast from recycled iron and seasoned by hand with flaxseed oil like everything we make.

What it's for

  • Sear-then-simmer dinners. Chicken thighs, short ribs, meatballs in sauce: brown on the stovetop, add liquid, lid on, low oven.
  • Rice and grains. The wide base cooks rice in an even layer and builds a crisp bottom crust.
  • Shakshuka and baked eggs. Serve straight from the pan at the table.
  • Anything a Dutch oven is too deep for. Less liquid, more browning.

Good to know

Bare cast iron and long-simmered acidic sauces (tomato, wine, vinegar) don't mix well in a brand-new pan: they can dull fresh seasoning and taste faintly metallic. Short cooks are fine. After a few months of regular use the seasoning is tough enough for a weeknight tomato braise. If a sauce ever strips a patch, re-season it or send it to us and we'll do it free.

The Glass Lid doesn't fit the Braiser; it's sized for 10-inch rims.
