Which one first?

Cooking for one or two: the 10-inch Skillet. Cooking for four or more, or you want to sear two steaks without crowding: the 12-inch Big Skillet. Braises, bread and big pots of soup: the Dutch Oven. Shallow braises, paella-style rice and one-pan chicken thighs: the Braiser. Everyone should own the Care Kit.
