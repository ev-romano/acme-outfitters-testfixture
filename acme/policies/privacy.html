Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We keep this short because our approach is simple: we collect what we need to sell you a pan and ship it, and nothing more.

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, shipping and billing address, email, and payment details. When you email us, we keep the message so we can help you. When you browse the site, we collect basic analytics — pages visited, rough location, device type — to understand what's working.

How we use it

To process and ship your order, answer your questions, handle returns and guarantee claims, and, if you opt in, send occasional email about new products or care tips. That's the whole list.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't rent your email list. We don't compile profiles on you across the web to resell.

Who we share it with

Only the vendors we need to run the shop: a payment processor to charge your card (we never store full card numbers ourselves), shipping carriers to deliver your order, and an email provider if you've subscribed. Each handles your data only to do that job.

Payment security

Card payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider over an encrypted connection. Your full card number never touches our servers.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in any message. You can ask us what data we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it — email hello@acme-outfitters.example and a person will handle it.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep your cart working and to measure site traffic. You can block cookies in your browser; the cart may not function fully if you do.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@acme-outfitters.example.
